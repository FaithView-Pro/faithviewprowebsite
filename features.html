Real-time scripture detection

FaithView Pro listens during the sermon and recognizes when a Bible verse is referenced or quoted — then puts the correct verse on screen automatically. Quoted passages and spoken references ("turn to Romans ten, seventeen") are both detected.

Detection is fast and accurate, verified before it ever hits the screen: every match is checked and shown to the operator, who can approve it with one click or dismiss it. Nothing reaches the projector by accident.

  • Works entirely offline, on your machine
  • Operator approves every verse before it shows
