Frequently asked

Does it work without internet?
Yes. FaithView Pro is offline-first — scripture detection, all 11 translations, playback, and output run entirely on your machine. You never need connectivity mid-service.
How many devices does one license cover?
The Standard license covers your church — every presentation machine you run services on. Signed activation keeps each machine verifiably licensed.
Which Bible translations are included?
Major Bible versions including KJV, NKJV, NLT, MSB, AMB, ESV , NASB others available
